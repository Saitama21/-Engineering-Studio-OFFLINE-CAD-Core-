ROZFOOD ENGINEERING STUDIO — third-party / interoperability references

SLDDRW modern chunk-container reader design was informed by:

1) SWFormat — KenM76/swformat
   License: Apache License 2.0
   https://github.com/KenM76/swformat
   Relevant public concepts: modern SOLIDWORKS chunk marker, ROL-obfuscated stream names,
   raw-DEFLATE stream payloads, SheetPreviews/SheetNames, Images/Sheet_N,
   swXmlContents/KeyWords and document-property streams.

2) openswx — schwitters/openswx
   License: MIT
   Referenced by SWFormat as prior art for the modern chunk walker.

This ROZFOOD browser implementation is read-only, operates on user-provided local files,
and does not decrypt protected/vault data or require SOLIDWORKS / Document Manager.
